How do I make my mount jump or attack?

Jump: press Space. The Lox, Asksvin and Moose have a hand-made jump, so you can climb ledges and rocks in a few jumps.

Attack: the attack key is the mount's light attack (Lox and Asksvin: Bite, Moose: Horns). The secondary attack key goes through its other attacks in turn (Lox: Stomp; Moose: Hooves, Horn sweep; Asksvin: Headbutt, Pounce).
