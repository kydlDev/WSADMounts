The mod seems to do nothing, what do I check?

1. Ride a tamed mount that has a saddle.
2. General > Enable Mod must be true. On a server the admin controls it.
3. General > Enable Custom Controls must be true.
4. KydlCore must be installed (the mod manager does it for you).
5. On a server, your WSADMounts version must match the server's exactly.

Still nothing? Turn on [DebugLog] Enable, ride for a moment and send BepInEx/LogOutput.log to the KydlDev Discord.
