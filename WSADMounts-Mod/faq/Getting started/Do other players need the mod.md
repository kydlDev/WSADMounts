Do other players need the mod too?

No. WSADMounts works on your own side, so you can use it on any server, also one without the mod. It also needs KydlCore, which the mod manager installs for you.

Players who have the mod too see your backward walk and your mount jump the way you set them; players without it see the game's normal forward walk while your mount moves backwards (like the Moonwalk style), not the mod's backward animation.
